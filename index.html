Climate-smart future for farming

Vertical farms that power themselves

GrowTech builds hydroponic vertical farms that run entirely on solar. Start with one module in your backyard or grow to a commercial farm. The farm beside this text is live: it is running its own day right now.

Explore the farmSee how it works
Step 1 · Power

The sun goes on top

Every module carries its own ~450 W solar panel as a roof. The power source uses no extra ground, and the shade keeps the top tier cooler.

~1,350 Wh a day*Tilted ~12° to the sun
Step 2 · Storage

All the power in one small cabinet

A lockable cabinet on the end of the frame holds the 24 V lithium battery, the charge controller and the ESP32 controller. Watch the yellow dots: energy flows from the panel into the battery and out to the farm.

24 V DC, no inverter2.56 kWh batteryAway from the water
Step 3 · Water

Water that never stops moving

The pump lifts nutrient water to every tier. It flows past the roots in the channels and drains back to the tank. No soil, and the same water goes round again.

Closed loopPump runs day and night
Step 4 · Night

Growing after dark

After sunset the battery takes over. LED grow lights extend the day and the pump keeps running. Drag the clock to see any hour.

Step 5 · Cloudy days

When the clouds stay, the plants still drink

If the battery runs low, the controller switches loads off in order. Fans go first, then the lights. The pump always keeps running.

Fans offbelow ~35%
LED lights offbelow ~20%
Pumpalways on
Step 6 · Scale

As big and as high as you want

Modules are built like scaffolding. Bolt them side by side or stack them upward. Each one brings its own panel, battery and controller, so the farm stays solar however large it gets.

One module is about 2.1 m × 1.1 m and 1.8 m high with 4 tiers (indicative).
