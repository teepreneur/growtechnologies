Water & nutrient flow

Salty raw water → RO → clean-water tank → a separate nutrient loop for each crop. Fish water goes one way to the tomatoes; brine goes to a soakaway.

Open sheet D-06

What goes where

From the entrance to the far end of the main greenhouse, plus the nursery house next to it.

Service & tank corner

RO plant and clean-water tank, tomato and lettuce nutrient tanks, control panel, 24 V battery backup.

Tomato

Drip-fed Dutch buckets on a 15 cm raised base, trained up to a 2.4 m high wire.

Lettuce

Vertical NFT A-frames with 5 channel levels per side.

Strawberry cool chamber

A clear chamber with its own AC and chilled root water, for cool nights and real flowering.

Teaching benches

Wick and Kratky demonstration systems for students, right next to the new entrance.

New KOPIA entrance

A new porch and door in the long side wall, opposite the nursery, with a bridge over the outside drain. A cross aisle leads to the central aisle. The old gable entrance stays, so there are two entrances.

Aquaponics

2,000 L tilapia tank, filters, gravel bed and three deep-water raft beds. Fish water also feeds the tomatoes.

Nursery house

Five 5 m seedling benches with misting and LED day-length lights.
